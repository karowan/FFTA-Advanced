# FFTA Advanced v0.7.4

This update fixes the pub menu after accepting a mission. Dismissing the payment
dialogue now returns to the Missions list, as it does in the original game. In
v0.7.3 that step opened Rumors. The mission assignment and gil charge already
worked and are unchanged.

Download **FFTA-Advanced-v0.7.4.zip** and apply its BPS patch to your own clean
Final Fantasy Tactics Advance (USA) ROM. The ZIP contains no ROM or emulator.

- Clean ROM SHA-1: `4ac05441f4de70a4ec3dd932116346c61b8783d9`
- Patched ROM SHA-1: `dd20c5771418cf470594c3e79c6d197b3bae4848`

Normal in-game saves from v0.7.0–v0.7.3 remain compatible. Back up your save and
load it through the game's Continue menu after updating. Do not carry an
emulator savestate across versions.

The change is one instruction in the accepted v0.7.3 game. A fixed-input replay
of an ordinary Dueling Sub dispatch confirmed the clean game and v0.7.4 return
to the Missions list while v0.7.3 opens Rumors. This focused test does not
establish a full campaign playthrough or physical GBA behavior.
