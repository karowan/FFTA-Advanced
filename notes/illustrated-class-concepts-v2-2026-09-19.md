# Revised illustrated concepts

The user liked the second-pass designs and requested restoring the first-pass
Samurai helmet crescent. The selected Samurai now has a gold crescent; its
second-pass red and black armor, teal sash and yellow shin wraps remain the design.
The other nine images were retained. This is concept direction, not native art
integration or approval of the newly edited crescent.

Each initial design used four original FFTA job illustrations for its race and
one or two other Final Fantasy class references. FFTA governs the drawing style;
the additional games provide recognizable job costume and equipment cues.
Bangaa Viking and Dark Knight deliberately differ in body shape, muzzle,
coloring, costume and equipment.

- Samurai: FFT Samurai.
- Dark Knights: FFIV Cecil and FFT War of the Lions Dark Knight.
- Viking: FFIII Viking.
- Chemists: FFT Chemists.
- Geomancer: FFIII and FFT Geomancers.
- Bard: FFT and FFIII Bards.
- Dancer: FFT Dancer.
- Mystic Knight: FFXI Rune Fencer.

Exact prompts, input images, attribution and hashes are recorded in the
[second-pass manifest](../src/art/race-study/illustrated-class-concepts-v2.json).
The crescent revision records both earlier Samurai images and their limited roles.
Original outputs remain intact; the gallery selects `human-samurai-crescent.png`.
