# Martial balance council report — proposal, not applied

> Historical council review of v0.4, adopted through the reconciled v0.5 specification. Statements about unchanged canonical files describe the review stage. For current rules, use [the class specification](../JOB-CLASS-SPECIFICATION.md), [axe addendum](../AXE-SKILL-EXPANSION.md), and [adoption record](../BALANCE-COUNCIL.md).

September 14, 2026. Review of version 0.4. Scope: all 32 Samurai/Dark Knight/Viking/Mystic Knight actions, their four combo entries, and all eight Soldier/Gladiator axe actions. Canonical design files and ROM are unchanged. Values below are starting points for measurement, not verified engine mappings or playtested balance.

## Judgment and researched benchmark

Mystic Knight needs a structural change before numerical tuning. Its current eight self-only preparations spend a full action to improve later Fight attacks, while those attacks remain vulnerable to Fight-blocking reactions. Dark Knight pays too much for attacks that other commands can approximate without sacrifice; its setup and recovery turns further reduce pressure. Samurai has the most coherent existing martial kit. Viking needs distinct storm choices and faithful theft resolution rather than three escalating lightning spells plus doubly gated theft.

Vanilla mechanics provide a demanding baseline: Sonic Boom is a free range-four cross attack using ordinary weapon damage; Ultima Sword is listed at 300% weapon damage for 60 MP. Weapon Atk+ increases the underlying attack stat before defense. Steal Armor uses AType, not SType. New weapon techniques must not secretly receive Double Sword's second Fight strike. [Terence's mechanics research](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/26262).

Published optimized builds favor strong command combinations, accuracy, and fast units enabling allied turns. Paladin/Hunter and Red Mage/Summoner are useful ceiling benchmarks, but copying their strongest outcome into every new job would erase meaningful distinctions. [Optimal Unit Builds discussion](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/57520610).

Reflex and Strikeback answer qualifying Fight attacks, not every physical technique; Concentrate is unavailable to Bangaa. Bloodcasting and Weapon Atk+ cannot occupy the same support slot. [R/S Ability FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/39140). The original job command lists remain the legality baseline. [Ability FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/26226).

## Common proposal rules

- P means one primary-weapon physical damage reference after applicable defenses, never two weapons. Numeric multipliers operate on the reference, not on Weapon Attack. M values retain the draft's unverified magical power notation.
- All existing range, height, costs-before-hit, drain, undead, law, immunity, friendly-fire and no-counter-chain rules remain unless explicitly revised below. AP is the proposed mastery cost, not a level or unlock requirement.
- A means ordinary attack-style accuracy. S means ordinary status accuracy. Conditional effects use distinct previews. A status rider never bypasses immunity. Ranged reach is meaningful only if line-of-sight and legal tiles permit it.
- Default new damage techniques remain unable to crit, copy item status procs, trigger a second weapon, or enter Doublecast. Preserve existing vanilla abilities when used as a secondary.
- Compare early access against early available equipment, then compare mature builds at identical level, inherited stats and equipment tier. Do not balance an introductory axe against an endgame knight sword.

**Legal builds and limitations:** Samurai/Hunter can trade Iaido's centered single-target or healing roles against Hunt's strong weapon-based coverage. **Resource example, not a simulator result:** At 200 max HP and a 100-damage P reference, Blood Edge spends 20 HP for 150 pre-affinity damage. Sanguine Sword deals 95 and can recover 40, its cap, for 6 MP. Across two successful hits that is 245 damage and net 20 HP recovered. The opponent can interrupt, misses consume costs, and one attack must be a recovery strike; this is sustainable combat, not infinite free healing. Bloodcasting turns that 6 MP into 12 HP, lowering net recovery to 8. The same support cannot also supply Desperation or Weapon Atk+.

**High-risk stack to test:** At a fixed point in the HP cycle, Last Resort x1.25 and version 0.4 Desperation x1.30 (not the separate support panel's replacement) produce x1.625 eligible damage before other effects. Blood Edge can therefore reach 2.4375P before affinity if its HP cost crosses the threshold. That is intentional burst requiring HP exposure and prior setup, but compare it against equal-speed ranged safer builds. Human Damage > MP and Bangaa Dragonheart can reduce the intended defensive risk; they need explicit adversarial tests. Never use printed slow job growth as the only safeguard, since characters can have faster inherited stats and receive allied extra turns.

## Mystic Knight: an enchantment action must be useful immediately

Change all eight actions to have two targeting modes within the same action, not two actions or a free move. **Self mode:** pay MP and apply the enchantment. **Strike mode:** select an r1 enemy, pay MP, apply the enchantment even if the strike misses, then deliver one immediate physical A-type enchanted strike. This strike is a new technique, not Fight: no Double Sword, weapon proc, critical, reaction counter-chain or Doublecast. It retains ordinary applicable defensive reactions but is not blocked solely for being an ordinary Fight command. Future enchantment benefit remains limited to the primary Fight strike and persists under the existing dispel/weapon/status rules.

Keep primary rapier/saber and Silence gates. Applying a new enchantment replaces, rather than adds to, the old one. A target's absorption or immunity applies normally. No existing secondary, reaction or combo gets an enchantment rider. Set a single explicit Spellweave category for these mixed actions during integration; recommended **magic category** for both modes because the player selects an incanted enchantment action, although the immediate hit uses physical stats/defense. This is an explicit classification, not an invitation to multiply both categories on one action. Enchanted strike followed by Red Magic is Magic-to-Magic and receives no alternation bonus; Fight or a physical Lunge action can alternate with either. A separate Fury damage charge may recognize the physical damage formula of an enchanted strike, but its sequencing category still remains Magic and is evaluated only once.

**Why this fits the job:** The character commits magic to a weapon and uses it immediately when already engaged. It still wants sustained weapon combat afterward. There is no unrelated spell-nuke kit, sigil inventory, free action, or requirement to copy an exact FFV implementation. A preparation turn while approaching is still available; charging into melee is not mandatory.

**Legal combinations:** Red Mage/Spellblade and Mystic Knight/Red Magic can use rapiers, alternate enchanted strikes with existing healing or eligible Doublecast magic, and select Concentrate or a different support. Dancer/Spellblade can use a rapier; its dances do not inherit enchantments. Fencer/Spellblade can use a rapier; Swarmstrike and other Lunge actions keep their original effects rather than acquiring a second status rider. Assassin and Summoner cannot execute Spellblade with their normal weapon permissions, even if a mastered command can be selected. An Assassin-trained character may switch to an eligible main job and carry its accumulated stats.

**Failure/abuse tests:** A 60% damage hit followed by a 50% status check gives only 30% joint success; S x0.5 gives 15%. These are illustrative inputs, not universal in-game rates. Poison/Silence need the stronger rider; Sleep remains reduced because it denies an action and may enable accurate follow-ups. Check Red Mage Concentrate builds separately. Against Reflex, the initial incanted strike remains an attack option but future ordinary Fight can still be blocked. Recasting costs MP and an action, so persistent enchantment is not a free answer to every defense. Drain never heals from allies or absorbed damage. Flare's defense bypass must be measured at low and high target defense rather than described as a flat 25% damage bonus.

## Required evaluation matrix before claiming balance

1. **Three equipment/growth bands:** first plausible unlock, midcampaign, endgame; ordinary mixed leveling and deliberately optimized inherited stats separately. Record exact stats, weapon tier, support and reaction instead of relying on printed job names.
2. **Three enemy profiles:** ordinary weak target; physical or magical specialist with relevant defenses; boss/elite with its actual immunity set. Do not silently make all bosses immune to every status or assume none are.
3. **Action economy:** total damage, prevented hostile actions, party healing turns, and resources across first one, two and four personal actions. Count the ally's buff/heal/Smile/Quicken action cost. Compare stationary actions and repeated movement where turn-order rules differ.
4. **Cross-job ceilings:** Ninja/Iaido with Concentrate versus Double Sword; Samurai/Hunt; Human Dark Knight/Blue Magic with Bloodcasting + Damage > MP; Bangaa axe Gladiator/Dark Arts with Last Resort; Viking/Gladiator with Weapon Atk+ versus Half MP; Red Mage/Spellblade with Concentrate and original Doublecast spells; Fencer/Spellblade and Dancer/Spellblade.
5. **Adversarial behavior:** repeated drain against nearly dead/undead/absorbing targets; self-cost at 1 HP; stolen defenses and lethal follow-up; one item stolen once; Sleep applied after damage; reflected storm; dispelled enchantment; weapon change; reaction immunity; friendly fire; laws; damage caps and support stacking.
6. **Success criterion:** each action has a plausible ordinary-use case or explicit early/economic role, each job supports at least two legal builds, and no new setup requires repeated low-value turns merely to become average. Every new job need not beat the strongest vanilla exploit in direct damage, but it should earn its action without requiring enemies to cooperate.

## Decisions for council reconciliation

- Approve the immediate-strike Spellblade structure before fine-tuning its numbers. The alternate outcome of only raising enchantment proc rates leaves the initial lost action and Fight-only defense problem intact.
- Decide whether Last Resort's optional any-weapon strike is acceptable. It deliberately makes Dark Arts a useful partial secondary on axe jobs, while preserving sword gates on drain/sacrifice moves.
- Reconcile revised drains and Dark Mind with the separate Bloodcasting/reaction review. Their HP-positive action economy is intentional; an action-free healing or reaction loop is not.
- Reconcile War Cry's ailment protection with Bard/Chemist cures and party prevention. Its self-centered cross and limited three-condition cure avoid replacing full Esuna from range.
- Reconcile Tsunami displacement with Geomancer fields, height rules and Sea Legs. No collision damage is proposed; illegal displacement leaves the victim in place without negating damage.


## Peer-review notes for the chair

The support/reaction panel's Desperation is 1.50 at 40% HP, unlike the historical 1.30 example above. With Last Resort and allied Battle Chant, Blood Edge can reach 3.375P and Unholy Sacrifice 3.9375P per target before affinity. Recommend starting the 1.50 bonus at 35% HP instead: this avoids automatically qualifying immediately after a 50%-HP Dragonheart revival plus Blood Edge's 10%-max-HP payment. The larger 20%-HP sacrifice still deliberately crosses that threshold.

Follow Through + Last Resort + allied Battle Chant gives axe Gladiator/Dark Arts Fell Cleave 3.645P, while Last Resort and Exposed multiply incoming physical damage to 1.44. A legal Dancer/Spellblade Sword Dance can reach 3.4992P with Spellweave, Fury, and allied Battle Chant after previous magic plus an enemy hit.
