# Council balance proposal: Geomancer, Chemist, Bard, Dancer

> Historical council review of v0.4, adopted through the reconciled v0.5 specification. Statements about unchanged canonical files describe the review stage. For current rules, use [the class specification](../JOB-CLASS-SPECIFICATION.md), [axe addendum](../AXE-SKILL-EXPANSION.md), and [adoption record](../BALANCE-COUNCIL.md).

September 14, 2026. Review of version 0.4. These are untested recommendations, not canonical edits or a ROM patch. This report covers all 32 assigned active IDs and all four combo IDs. Preserve the approved roster, races, prerequisites, and vanilla abilities. Read alongside the other council reports before adopting numbers.

## Findings and benchmark

The largest structural issue is Song's instrument requirement: under the listed equipment permissions, only Bard can equip instruments. Almost the entire command therefore fails as a secondary on another Moogle job. Remove the instrument requirement from active songs; keep Silence as a real weakness. Instruments remain Bard's equipment and combo requirement.

Chemist needs both practical ranged medicine and a few actual preparations. Eight AP-gated copies of ordinary items become difficult to justify once Long Throw makes ordinary Item ranged. Merge the narrow cures, group high-grade tonics, and use the freed lessons for a healing mist and stronger revival mixture. Do not make rare Ethers silently shop-buyable.

Dancer's single-target 15% debuffs and fixed Attack 12/18 dance formulas have weak action value. Give focused weakening dances damage plus a meaningful debuff; scale the virtual weapon by level; make its flagship tempo control an area effect. Bard likewise needs useful group buffs and MP recovery, rather than spending 6 MP and one action to transfer 8 MP.

Vanilla benchmark facts: Smile grants an ally an immediate turn at range 4 without MP; Quicken costs 24 MP at range 3. Haste costs 24 MP and targets one unit. Slow is already a range-3 area spell costing 12 MP. Cura is range 4 with an area and costs 10 MP. Ordinary Potion/Hi-Potion/X-Potion restore 25/50/150 HP; Ether restores 80 MP and is not a normal shop purchase. Combo participation has separate power, range, and accuracy profiles. These facts and the attack/equipment distinction come from [Terence's original mechanics research](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/26262). They are benchmarks, not proof that proposed custom handlers work.

The [optimal-unit discussion](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/57520610) emphasizes fast turn-support Moogles, Doublecast Summoners, and efficient offense. The proposed jobs should earn inclusion in that environment without requiring every ordinary support action to exceed a perfected endgame attack. Compare both organically leveled and deliberately optimized parties.

## Common rules for these recommendations

Unless a row overrides it, retain version 0.4 height, legal-target, LOS, immunity, resource, duration, and non-Doublecast rules. AP values below are complete proposed values, including unchanged values. M powers remain provisional custom magical-power references, not verified ROM fields. Pdance is defined below. Buff percentages multiply final eligible damage, not Attack/Magic Power stats. No new action grants another action, changes CT directly, refunds its action, or gives a free support slot.

Named vanilla statuses retain vanilla durations. Custom damage bonuses and weakening use T2. Repeated applications refresh rather than stack. Different named buffs may combine: that is intended party synergy, subject to the burst tests below. A dispel removes a beneficial custom buff; an appropriate broad remedy removes a harmful custom modifier. Damage-triggered riders occur after the initiating damage and cannot enhance that same hit.

## Legal build and adversarial tests

1. **Nu Mou Geomancer/Time Mage:** choose field or exposure, then use Time Magic as a separate later action. Equip one support such as Turbo MP or Surefoot, never both. Check whether nature actions' existing classification actually receives Turbo MP's power/accuracy/cost behavior; do not infer from having an MP cost alone. Primary Geomancer's modest Speed means whole-battle control must justify its timing.
2. **Nu Mou Alchemist/Geomancy:** use ordinary magic development and a useful secondary control toolkit; versus **Geomancer/Chemist** for silence-proof emergency items. Alchemist is an important vanilla exception: its fixed ordinary Item command exists alongside its primary and secondary sets. Therefore **Alchemist/Sagacity + Long Throw** is a serious alternative to Chemist, retaining two learned action sets plus ranged ordinary medicine. The new mixtures, not merely ranged Potion, must justify choosing Chemist. This fixed extra command does not grant a third learned set to other jobs. MP recovery still needs actual Ether stock or an allied Bard. The Alchemist exception is documented in [DarthMarth's job FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/26178).
3. **Moogle Juggler/Song:** after removing the active instrument gate, Smile and group songs become legal choices. A song should win when it benefits several allied actions; Smile should remain competitive for an immediate decisive attack. A Thief/Song has songs and Thievery, not Smile. A Juggler/Chemist has Stunt and medicine, not Song.
4. **Moogle Bard/Chemist:** sustained group support plus practical supplies, but no Smile or Time Magic in the same two-slot build. Lower physical output and finite medicines still matter. Test clear-voice support against other equipped supports rather than assuming free immunity.
5. **Viera Assassin/Dance:** bows must not make virtual dances use bow power or weapon procs. Sword Dance is unavailable with a bow, but the remainder stays usable. **Red Mage/Dance** permits Doublecast only for eligible magic in its own legal command access, never dances. **Dancer/White Mage** gains healing at the expense of another offensive command; accumulated Magic Power still controls its ordinary spells.
6. **Wisp plus Inspired Magic plus a Doublecast Summoner:** 1.25 exposure x 1.20 song = 1.50 magical damage before other distinct effects; requires two allied setup actions and a vulnerable target. Compare total three-unit-turn damage with simply attacking or Smiling the Summoner. Use realistic order, range, target survival, MP, and loss of buffs on KO/dispel. Keep this synergy unless actual evidence shows universal dominance.
7. **Battle Chant plus new physical supports:** multiplicative buffs can aid Samurai/Dark Knight/Assassin attackers. Measure final damage instead of adding percentages. Reactions and combos get no new outgoing buff here, avoiding unpriced off-turn replication.
8. **Bard battery and extra turns:** run two-Bard and Bard+Time Mage+Juggler schedules, counting every donated and productive action. Ballad's 20 MP is below Quicken's full ordinary price but existing resources/supports can bridge the difference; the test is net productive actions, not simply whether MP rises while stalling.
9. **Healing Mist plus Pharmacology plus recipient Recuperation:** at 300 max HP the current proposed chain yields floor(60 x 1.5 x 1.25)=112 HP on the Human recipient with Recuperation. Item user's support and recipient's support occupy separate units' slots. No repeated application, cure-trigger feedback, or inventory consumption per target.
10. **Field lattice:** multiple Geomancers plus allied Float can shape a corridor; fields do not stack movement costs, block all paths, create affinity, or cause entry damage. Record time spent placing fields versus turns actually denied. Faster casters also expire their own fields sooner under the chosen timer.
11. **Debuff-resistant boss:** Polka/Heathen should keep baseline damage when a custom effect is immune. Do not blanket-immune every boss without a counterplay reason. Conversely, damage-reduction effects must not reduce scripted fixed damage or costs they do not claim to affect.
12. **Campaign learning:** run levels roughly 10/25/40 at actual shop tiers, not all lessons and 46-Attack gear immediately. Compare naturally leveled Dancer/Geomancer with Assassin/Thief/caster-trained imports. Chemist healing is stat-independent, so the Moogle version's greater Speed has inherent utility advantage; Nu Mou magic growth matters only to its secondary spells. This is a deliberate racial distinction, not justification to weaken both races' medicine.

## Acceptance criteria before calling the pass balanced

Every active has at least one ordinary encounter where choosing it is sensible, without obliging a maximum-stat endgame build to use all eight. Compare three-target and single-target cases, early equipment and final equipment, immunity, missing ingredients, Silence, bad terrain, and unequal turn order. Record useful damage/healing/control per party action and battle resources, not only per successful hit. Require preview/actual-effect agreement and verify new formulas on clean-ROM-derived test cases before final numerical tuning. Nothing in this council report establishes those tests have passed.

## Second-round reconciliation

The support/reaction panel proposes Recuperation x1.50. If adopted, the earlier 300-HP Healing Mist example changes from 112 to **135 HP**: 60 base x 1.50 caster Pharmacology x 1.50 recipient Recuperation. The two supports remain on different characters. The example of 112 above explicitly described the prior x1.25 support and is not the council's reconciled figure.

With the support panel's Desperation x1.50, martial Last Resort x1.25, and Battle Chant x1.20, combined eligible physical damage is **x2.25**. Blood Edge at 1.50P reaches **3.375P**, or legal axe Fell Cleave at 1.80P reaches **4.05P**, before affinity and other distinct effects. Low HP, actual accuracy, party setup, incoming-risk modifiers, learned command slots, and action costs still matter. Desperation excludes simultaneous Concentrate, Weapon Atk+, or Bloodcasting. Treat this as a high-priority burst test, not a proved reason to prohibit the build. Wisp x1.25 and Inspired Magic x1.20 remain x1.50 together.

Spellblade's enchant-and-strike can be tagged magic for Spellweave sequencing while dealing physical damage. **Battle Chant**, not Magickal Refrain or Wisp Exposure, therefore affects its strike. Sequence classification must never select the damage stat/defense or grant both damage buffs. Drain recovery is calculated from actual boosted damage once, then capped; excluding a separate drain-healing bonus does not erase the legitimate damage increase.

Encore's reactive self-Haste cannot trigger Encouragement. Nameless Song's three statuses trigger one Encouragement heal per other ally, never three. Reapplication while the status already exists produces no bonus healing; spending a later action to apply a genuinely absent buff is allowed. No automatic healing loop follows from these rules.

**Adopted Encouragement classification:** exclude immediate turn-grant/CT effects such as Smile and Quicken from the qualifying persistent-beneficial-status set. Otherwise every already valuable free Smile could additionally deliver the proposed 15%-max-HP/cap-60 heal, strongly favoring a universal Juggler sustain build. The chair adopted this exclusion; it is not a claim that such a loop already exists in an implementation.

For Magick Boost, recommend the charged x1.30 enhance the selected song's own HP-restoration component, not the separately triggered Encouragement bonus. At 300 max HP, charged Angelsong then restores 78 HP plus 45 Encouragement before incoming modifiers; on a Human with x1.50 Recuperation the two separately resolved heal events restore 117 + 67 = **184 HP**, capped by missing HP. A refresh loses the Encouragement component. This is strong group rescue paid for by enemy damage/survival, an R slot, an S slot, and the song action. It does not produce a second song or reaction. The chair should specify rounding per resolved healing event consistently.
