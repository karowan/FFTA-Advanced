# Balance council: supports and reactions

> Historical council review of v0.4, adopted through the reconciled v0.5 specification. Statements about unchanged canonical files describe the review stage. For current rules, use [the class specification](../JOB-CLASS-SPECIFICATION.md), [axe addendum](../AXE-SKILL-EXPANSION.md), and [adoption record](../BALANCE-COUNCIL.md).

September 14, 2026. Independent council proposal against design 0.4. Covers all 18 S and all 18 R entries. No canonical specification or ROM changed. Exact numbers below are starting points for the shared balance pass, not measured balance claims.

## Position

The support design has several viable niches; it does not need eighteen replacements for Concentrate. The reactions have a systemic problem: chance, narrow trigger, survival, equipment conditions, weak payoff, and a turn lock frequently apply together. Remove the blanket turn lock and make qualifying reactions dependable. Retain ordinary reaction incapacity and prevent reaction chains.

The target is a credible reason to equip a lesson on a legal build at its acquisition tier. A cheap terrain or economy lesson may remain situational. Do not inflate every number until it beats the strongest vanilla exploit.

## Verified vanilla anchors

Terence's reverse-engineered [mechanics guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/26262), checked September 14, 2026: Concentrate reduces Evade by 50 for A-type accuracy and SRes by 20 for status accuracy. Turbo MP reduces them by 15/10, including zero-MP actions; its attack-stat bonus requires an MP-costing ability. Weapon Atk+/Magic Pow+ multiply the applicable attack stat by 307/256; Turbo MP uses 332/256. Protect/Shell multiply the corresponding defense stat by 358/256. These are not final-damage percentages. Facing changes ordinary Evade to full/half/quarter. Ordinary caps and immunities still apply. The guide acknowledges unfinished edge cases; verify handlers in ROM before implementation.

The [R/S Ability FAQ](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gba/560436-final-fantasy-tactics-advance/faqs/39140), checked September 14, 2026, establishes these crucial access boundaries:

| Vanilla lesson | Racial access |
|---|---|
| Concentrate | Human, Moogle, Viera |
| Turbo MP | Human, Nu Mou, Viera |
| Weapon Atk+ | Human, Bangaa |
| Half MP | Human, Bangaa, Nu Mou, Viera |
| Immunity | Human, Nu Mou |
| Damage > MP | Human, Nu Mou, Moogle |
| Reflex | Human, Bangaa, Nu Mou, Viera |
| Strikeback | Human, Bangaa |
| Dragonheart | Bangaa |
| Return Magic | Human, Bangaa, Nu Mou, Moogle |

Reflex answers Fight, not all physical abilities. Strikeback additionally needs its own Fight range. Damage > MP and Dragonheart have qualifying A-type HP-damage conditions. Return Magic has command/target-pattern restrictions; it is not every magical-damage effect. Auto-Life, reaction prevention, and redirection must use their actual handlers. The R/S guide's Immunity list disagrees with the mechanics guide over Silence/Slow; use the latter for effect mapping and verify in game.

## Global proposed rules

1. One equipped S, one R, two action commands. Learned lessons never transfer race or equipment permissions. Only one reaction can answer an incoming action. A multi-hit or multi-cast enemy action is one incoming action for new reaction scheduling; resolve at most one retaliation/recovery after the complete action. Pre-damage reduction applies consistently to all eligible components of that action.
2. **Remove the global until-next-turn reaction lock.** Each new reaction below instead states its own lock, if any. Eligibility alone never consumes a lock. Defensive interception happens after a successful hit check, before the relevant damage/status is applied; it cannot spend an item, MP, enchantment, or gil on a miss.
3. Unless expressly an interception, the unit must survive the whole triggering action and remain able to react. No self/ally damage, HP payment, poison, combos, other reactions, or reflected reaction chain triggers. A single counter must target the original legal attacker; no retargeting.
4. Unless stated otherwise, damage modifiers operate on final direct HP damage, not WAtk/MPow. Existing exclusions for costs, fixed/percentage damage, instant death, items, drain recovery, DoT and combos remain. Bonuses to a multi-target action apply per recipient but are established only once per action. Explicit exceptions below supersede these exclusions.
5. Derived statuses are non-stacking. Reapplying Fury/Magick Boost replaces the charge and timer; it never banks charges or raises stats permanently. KO/Petrify/battle end/removing the lesson clears custom state. T2 retains the main specification's definition.
6. AP changes must update teaching items and acquisition plans. AP is a progression lever, not a late-game excuse for weak mechanics. No extra lesson or replacement job is introduced.

## Provisional acceptance measurements

Use matched equipment/stat histories around levels10,25,40 and early/mid/late lesson access. For each credible build compare six useful turns with the best accessible vanilla support/reaction, not only a single maximum hit. Report actions spent on setup/healing, expected hit-adjusted HP damage, ally actions enabled, resources consumed, survival under two-enemy focus fire, and whether an enemy can meaningfully answer it. Include neutral, weakness, resistant, status-immune, and high-evasion targets; short battles and long sustain fights.

Concrete sanity checks: old Absorb Damage returned only0.35×0.10 =3.5% expected lostHP; the proposed30% is reliable but cannot save a lethal hit. Old Blade Ward averaged12% mitigation before its lock; proposed35% has a weapon gate and no Fight immunity. Old Follow Through's15% conditional final damage could lose to a20% **stat** bonus even when active; proposed35% requires real relocation and still needs formula comparisons. These are our calculations, not additional claims from the external sources.

Highest-risk provisional proposals: Bloodcasting + Damage > MP, Desperation + Auto-Life, Grace with maximum evasion gear, Encore on a fast Smile unit, and Spell Parry if enchantment also performs a full attack. These deserve focused scenario testing before normal playthrough testing. Numeric changes here are a council candidate, not permission to silently rewrite the vanilla game or strengthen enemies.

## Peer-review reconciliation

Read the completed martial, magic/utility, and chair reports. Gil Snapper remains a cheap economy niche after chair adjudication. Spell Parry consumes its enchantment for50% physical reduction, rather than complete negation, because the new enchantment action also attacks. The support panel's Spellweave category agrees with the martial panel: both enchantment modes establish magic once; an immediate physical strike is not a second sequence event.

The chair retains Desperation's1.50 multiplier but restores the≤35%HP threshold: Auto-Life's50% restoration followed by one10% Blood Edge payment otherwise reaches40% and enables the full bonus too easily. Fury reads physical damage independently of Spellweave's sequencing tag. Encouragement explicitly excludes immediate turn/CT effects, including Smile and Quicken.

The utility panel's recipes and songs introduce these explicit arithmetic checks:

- At300 recipient maxHP, Healing Mist with Pharmacology from its user and revised Recuperation on a Human recipient yields60×1.5×1.5=135HP. The utility panel's earlier112 figure used the old1.25 Recuperation. It does not apply to revival and consumes the recipe only once.
- At300 target maxHP, a charged Bard using Angelsong with Encouragement grants60×1.30+45=123 immediate HP to a newly Regen-buffed ally. A Human's revised Recuperation changes that to floor(123×1.5)=184, plus ordinary Regen. The Bard spent its S and R, had to survive damage for the charge, and only the new status grants Encouragement. Already-active Regen removes the45 bonus. This is a strong group rescue to measure; it is not a recursive healing trigger.
- Bangaa Dark Knight with Desperation, Last Resort, allied Battle Chant and Unholy Sacrifice can reach1.75×1.50×1.25×1.20=3.9375P per target before accuracy/affinity at14MP plus20%maxHP. Last Resort and the song use setup actions, but Last Resort itself attacks. Dragonheart can soften the subsequent danger. This is the strongest burst concern: compare whole-party actions and survivor risk against vanilla Ultima rather than approving the number from single-class output alone.
- Polka plus Poise plus Blade Ward can multiply a qualifying enemy's physical final damage by.65×.75×.65=.316875, before ordinary Protect-derived defense. That uses a Dancer action plus the Human's S and R and a katana; it is legal layered protection, not three lessons on one slot. Check focus fire and custom-debuff immunity.

No additional global caps or exclusions are proposed merely to suppress these combinations. If tests show universal dominance, first tune the specific enabling magnitude or acquisition, preserving the job-mixing interaction.
